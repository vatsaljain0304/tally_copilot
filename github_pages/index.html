How to Use This Testing Console (Simple 2-Step Guide)

  1. Option A: Upload Tally XML (No Installation Needed):
    • In TallyPrime, open Day Book (or Display > Day Book).
    • Press Alt + E (Export) > Format: XML (Data Interchange) > Save file to your Desktop.
    • Click the blue 📁 Upload Tally XML button above and select your file. All your cash runways and debtor predictions will appear instantly!
  2. Option B: 1-Click Live Connection:
    • Double-click 1-Click-Run.bat on the computer where Tally is open.
    • Your browser will pop open automatically with everything connected.
  3. Testing & WhatsApp Sending:
    • Review Cash Runway and debtor predictions.
    • If any prediction differs from customer reality, click "⚠️ Discrepancy" on that row to record it.
    • In WhatsApp Actions, click "Send via WhatsApp Web" to test sending a reminder.
    • When finished, switch to Final Audit Report and click "Download Report" to share your feedback.
